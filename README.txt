TERRASLOTH STORE — EXPANDED EDITION

This is an expanded, responsive website preview with larger product cards and shirt photos, more breathing room, and phone-friendly controls.

HOW TO OPEN ON A PHONE
1. Extract this ZIP in your phone's Files app.
2. Open the Terrasloth_Store folder.
3. Tap index.html and open it in your browser.
4. Keep the assets folder beside index.html so product photos load.

The store is a local preview, not published online. Cart and checkout are demos; no real payments or orders are processed. Product prices for creature shirts are sample prices; the Terrasloth Original Mascot Tee is listed at $25.99.
